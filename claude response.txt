## Where I disagree

**1. Your "4 agents" are really a fixed pipeline.**
I disagree because Intake → Verifier → Risk → Action always runs in the same order, and Risk is plain Python. The judges are scoring a real agent (30%). Here is what I'd do instead: use one LLM that picks the next tool itself (Crustdata company lookup, Crustdata people lookup, domain check, UPI check). It should re-plan when the evidence conflicts. The risk in your approach is that it scores like a script with an LLM wrapped around it.

**2. Drop XGBoost.**
I disagree because you have no training data. **[Likely]** Judges will ask what it was trained on. Use a weighted rule score and show the evidence list. This also saves you hours.

**3. Don't make Tesseract the main OCR.**
Tesseract is weak on WhatsApp screenshots with Hindi text. Use a vision model through LiteLLM first and keep Tesseract as the fallback. That fallback becomes your natural recovery story.

**4. Don't mock Crustdata in the demo.**
You have 5000 free credits, so use real calls and cache the responses. A mocked tool hurts the "works (literally)" theme. **[Guessing]** Crustdata may not know tiny Indian startups, so add an "unable to verify" verdict. That honest answer will impress judges more than a fake-confident one.
